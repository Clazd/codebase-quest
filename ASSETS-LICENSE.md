## Third-party assets: their own licenses

Some assets come from other creators and keep their own licenses. Every one of
them is listed, with its source, author and license, in
[`packages/assets/LICENSES.md`](packages/assets/LICENSES.md).

We only accept third-party assets under CC0, CC-BY or compatible licenses.
Never non-commercial (NC) or no-derivatives (ND) licenses, and never ripped
assets.

## AI-assisted assets

AI-assisted assets are accepted only after real human or code rework, and they
must be disclosed in `packages/assets/LICENSES.md`. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#art-and-audio).
